The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2012
In accordance with section 7(2)(ha) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000 , the Secretary of State has consulted the Electoral Commission.
Citation, commencement and interpretation
1
1
This Order may be cited as the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2012.
2
This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.
3
“Police area” means a police area listed in Schedule 1 to the Police Act 1996 (police areas outside London).
Designation of police area returning officers
2
1
This article designates for the purposes of section 54(1) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (returning officers etc ) the person who is to be police area returning officer for —
a
each police area in England other than Lancashire and Sussex, and
b
each police area in Wales other than Gwent.
2
In respect of a police area in England listed in column 1 of Schedule 1, the police area returning officer is to be the person who is acting returning officer for the parliamentary constituency listed in column 2 of the Schedule opposite to the name of the police area (being a constituency that falls wholly or partly within the police area) .
3
In respect of a police area in Wales listed in column 1 of Schedule 2, the police area returning officer is to be the person who is acting returning officer for the parliamentary constituency listed in column 2 of the Schedule opposite to the name of the police area (being a constituency that falls wholly or partly within the police area) .
